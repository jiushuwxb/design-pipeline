# Premium Bento SaaS

Use this style for polished SaaS landing pages, AI product pages, and feature showcases.

## Design Intent
- First viewport should make the product/category obvious immediately.
- Use bento-style feature blocks with mixed card sizes and clear information hierarchy.
- Pair concise marketing copy with concrete product UI, screenshots, metrics, or workflow previews.
- Feel premium and modern without relying on generic purple-blue gradient overload.

## Layout Rules
- Hero: centered or left-aligned headline, compact supporting copy, primary CTA, secondary proof signal.
- Keep a hint of the next section visible in the first viewport.
- Use 12-column desktop grids and 1-column mobile stacks.
- Feature sections should alternate between product proof, benefit explanation, and metric evidence.

## Component Recipes
- Buttons: 8px radius, clear icon+label when useful, high contrast primary action.
- Cards: 8px radius max, subtle border, soft shadow only for elevation.
- Bento cells: combine title, one sentence, icon/visual, and optional metric.
- Navigation: slim, sticky optional, no oversized logo treatment.

## Motion Rules
- Use small reveal transitions, smooth card hover elevation, and subtle product UI movement.
- Avoid constant looping motion unless it communicates product state.

## Negative Rules
- No vague glassmorphism panels everywhere.
- No decorative blobs/orbs.
- No card-inside-card layouts.
- No hero that hides the actual product/category signal.

## References
- Inspired by common modern SaaS, Framer, Webflow, and premium product marketing patterns.
